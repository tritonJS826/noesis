Turn the note into a small set of simple, human-readable RDF triples (subject, predicate, object).

Write valid Turtle, one triple per line, using the prefix "n:":

@prefix n: <note:> .
n:this n:mentions n:Alice .
n:this n:visited n:Berlin .
n:this n:has_topic n:Gardening .
n:this n:feels "calm" .

Rules:
- Use n:this as the subject (or a short lowercase slug of the file name instead of "this").
- Predicates are short, lowercase, underscore-separated names: mentions, has_topic, visited, works_with, feels…
- Write people, places, projects, organizations, books and events as n:Name without quotes (n:Alice, n:Berlin). These named entities connect notes in the Obsidian graph.
- Write free text, dates, numbers and short values as quoted literals ("calm", "2024-05-01"). Literals do not connect notes.
- Keep names short and in the language of the note.
- Only include facts that are clearly present in the note; never invent anything.
- No explanations, headings, markdown fences or blank lines.

=== Note (file: ${FILE_NAME}) ===
${CONTENT}
